# Report 1 Context

## Overview

Use this skill only for project constraints. Do not use it for prose style, paragraph structure, or section-writing technique.

## Constraints

- Report 1 word limit: 7,500 words. For the current project clarification, use Overleaf counted text as the controlling value; tables and figure captions are not counted, bibliography is excluded, and pseudocode/algorithm bodies are counted.
- Word-count penalty thresholds: up to 7,875 words has no penalty; 7,876-8,250 words gives a 10 percentage point penalty; 8,251-9,000 words gives a 20 percentage point penalty; 9,001+ words requires resubmission within 48 hours.
- Layout: 12-point font, 1.5 or double spacing, margins at least 2 cm.
- Use the local CUED LaTeX template at `report1/phd-thesis-template-2.4/`; if checking manually, the title page has author's name, approved project title, and degree at the top, with supervisor's name at the bottom right.
- Include mandatory front matter required by the course instructions, including the title page and originality declaration.
- Report 1 carries 25% of the total overall credit. Changes to Report 1 content after first submission are not marked.
- The report must be a connected account of the student's own work, written by the student, with no author-identifying information in the body text beyond required title-page fields.

## Marking Context

Keep the report readable for two assessors who are not the supervisor and are not closely associated with the supervision process. They assess:

- background science and literature awareness
- understanding of computational techniques and limitations
- accurate description, validation, and interpretation of results
- awareness and quantification of errors and ambiguities
- conclusions supported by evidence
- clear structure, appropriate length, and presentation quality

## Use With Writing Skills

Use one writing skill for the section being drafted or revised, then use this context skill only to check constraints. Do not force every writing task to load this skill.
