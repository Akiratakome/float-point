\chapter{Supplementary MCA Scope}
\label{sec:appendix-ch5-mca-status}

Table~\ref{tab:appendix-ch5-mca-status} separates the Monte Carlo arithmetic
(MCA) scopes discussed in Chapter~5. The p53 and p24 labels denote virtual
precisions selected in Verificarlo rather than IEEE fp64 and fp32 arithmetic.
Brio--Wu is the only matched solver comparison at a common resolution, end
time and sample count. The remaining completed rows provide limited evidence
about reduced-scope behaviour or toolchain validation; because their cases,
resolutions, end times or sample counts differ, their results are not pooled.
