sensitivity study.

## Manuscript chapter map

1. Introduction and Project Transition
2. Project Development: Ideal-MHD Solver
3. Experimental Design and Reproducibility Methodology
4. MHD Validation Results
5. Precision, Hardware, and Implementation Results
6. Discussion: Reproducibility Across Implementations
7. Conclusions and Future Work

The split is deliberate: Chapters 4 and 5 carry the 40% computational-results
weight, while Chapter 2 owns project development. Report 1 theory and Euler
validation are referenced only when they explain a Report 2 decision.
