\section{Contribution and claim boundaries}

The studied MUSCL--Hancock/GLM configurations completed with finite, positive
states, which supports stability over the tested horizons but not insensitivity
to arithmetic detail. The principal methodological contribution is an
experimental design organised by test case and controlled axis, which keeps
numerical-reference error, cross-variant discrepancy, stochastic spread and
runtime separate rather than treating them as interchangeable measures of
``accuracy''.

\section{Prioritised future work}

The first priority is to extend order verification beyond the one smooth case
that now carries it. The Alfv\'en wave should be joined by an inclined or
two-dimensional smooth problem and by a matched independent-code benchmark,
compared over density, momentum, total energy, magnetic field and derived
pressure with conservation residuals and a dimensionless divergence measure. A
cheaper step is a quadruple-precision one-dimensional Brio--Wu reference,
turning the present same-scheme $N=8000$ comparator into an arithmetic ground
truth and separating discretisation error from precision discrepancy without
new physics.

The next priority is to test the remaining mechanisms. An HLL variant without
the $\pm c_h$ clamp, solving the GLM pair separately, would separate the
Riemann solver from the wave-speed treatment that currently makes the tested
HLL a globally scaled Rusanov flux, and would show how much of its diffuse
discrepancy and low observed rates that clamp accounts for. The $512^2$
positivity failure should be repeated in fp32 to test whether its threshold
moves with the working precision. The build matrix should extend beyond the
single MSVC environment, so compiler, optimisation and floating-point semantics
can be distinguished rather than represented by one toolchain.

Execution coverage should then be made genuinely matched: GPU HLLD and
Kelvin--Helmholtz paths, complete matched Kelvin--Helmholtz temporal and MCA
experiments, actual OpenMP work sharing and distributed-memory Message Passing
Interface (MPI) reductions with controlled reduction orders, and repetition on
several recorded systems. Performance work should separate kernel, launch,
transfer and I/O costs and add hardware counters. Only then should the study
broaden to schemes with different dissipation and constraint treatment, or to
resistive or relativistic MHD.
