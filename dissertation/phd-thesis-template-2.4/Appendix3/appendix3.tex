\chapter{Execution Record and Evidence Locations}
\label{sec:appendix-provenance}

must carry the machine, toolchain and artefact record that produced it. This
appendix supplies that record for the present results.
Table~\ref{tab:appendix-platform} identifies the single workstation on which
every reported run was executed, together with the compilers and the device
flag that governs the matched CPU/GPU comparison of
Section~\ref{sec:ch4-cpu-gpu-validation}. All timing statements, and the
bitwise agreement results, are bound to this configuration.
