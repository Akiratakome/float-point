## Goal

Produce a natural, presentation-ready academic English version of the Week 15
supervisor report. The document should read as an English briefing rather than
a literal translation while preserving the source report's evidence and claim
boundaries.

## Content and structure

- Retain the source structure: overview, weekly work, six-figure walkthrough,
  supported and deferred claims, next steps, and references.
- Preserve every reported numerical value, experiment configuration, gate
  result, figure path, and solver/case name.
- Use concise academic English suitable both for speaking in a supervisor
  meeting and for reading as meeting notes.
- Explain specialist terms on first use where the source already provides
  enough context, without adding new scientific claims.
- Keep the distinction between engineering consistency, morphology validation,
  and exact-solution validation explicit.
- Describe the temporal-divergence result as motivation for the planned
  Lyapunov-like analysis, not as a completed exponent measurement.

## Constraints

- Do not modify the Chinese source document.
- Do not change solver numerics, configurations, experiment outputs, or figures.
- Do not strengthen causal language or claim that HLLD is production-ready.
- Do not claim GPU results, Kelvin–Helmholtz results, 512² results, or completed
  temporal-divergence fitting.

1. Compare all numbers and figure references against the Chinese source.
